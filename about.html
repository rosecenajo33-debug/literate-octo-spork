A LITTLE LOCAL KNOW-HOW

Make every journey
count.

The Philippines is a tropical archipelago with thousands of islands and many ways to explore. A little planning helps you spend less time worrying about logistics and more time enjoying the view.

BEFORE YOU GO

Travel tips for a
smoother trip.

01 / TIMING

Check the seasons

Weather varies by region. Research your destination's local forecast and seasonal conditions before booking, especially during the rainy and typhoon seasons.

02 / GETTING AROUND

Plan island transfers

Ferries and domestic flights connect many destinations. Check schedules ahead, allow buffer time, and confirm routes with official transport operators.

03 / PACKING

Pack light, pack smart

Bring breathable clothing, sun protection, a reusable water bottle, comfortable footwear, and a waterproof pouch for boat trips.

04 / RESPECT

Travel responsibly

Follow local guidelines, respect cultural sites, avoid touching marine life, and take all your rubbish with you.

FIND YOUR TRAVEL STYLE

There is no one way
to see the islands.

01 — SALT IN THE AIR

Ocean escapes

Spend your days island-hopping, kayaking through lagoons, snorkeling above colorful reefs, or simply watching the sun melt into the sea.

02 — STORIES & FLAVORS

Culture & heritage

Visit historic towns, explore local markets, try regional dishes, and learn from the communities who make each destination special.

03 — TAKE THE SCENIC ROUTE

Nature trails

Trade busy streets for mountain paths, waterfalls, forest walks, and quiet viewpoints. Choose marked routes and local guides where appropriate.
